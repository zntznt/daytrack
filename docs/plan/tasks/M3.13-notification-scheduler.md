## Goal
**Step 1 (spike).** Write `AppTests/Reminders/NotificationSpikeTests.swift` against the real `UNUserNotificationCenter` on the simulator. It must find out whether:
- 14 non-repeating `UNCalendarNotificationTrigger`s with `timeZone == nil` all appear in `pendingNotificationRequests()`;
- replacing them keeps the pending count at or below 64.

Record the results in `docs/spikes/notifications.md`, in no more than 20 lines, marked "verified on simulator only". **If either assumption fails, stop and report.**

**Simulator limit (found 2026-11-27):** on the iOS 27.2 simulator an app-hosted test cannot add requests to the real centre. `add` throws UNErrorDomain 2003 even with provisional authorization, and `simctl privacy` has no notifications service. So the two real-centre tests stay in the file but are disabled unless authorization is `.authorized`. Use Swift Testing `.enabled(if:)` so they run on a device. They are not named tests. Record this in the spike doc as "not verified; run on device at M3.15". Then do step 2 against the fake client. A failed spike is no longer a reason to stop.

**Step 2.** Build `NotificationScheduler: ReminderScheduling`, over a small `NotificationCenterClient` protocol:
- `replan` replaces every `reminder-YYYY-MM-DD` request with the `ReminderPlan`.
- It runs on launch, on foreground and after every write.
- The title is "Daily reminder".
- The category `daily` has two actions: **Open** (foreground) and **"Skip the rest of {dayLabel}"** (not foreground).
- The skip action reads the day from `userInfo`, goes through `Recorder.skipRest`, and writes nothing if that day is closed.
- After every replan, write DeviceState `remindersScheduledThrough` (the last planned day, or nil when the plan is empty). M8.01 reads it.
- **Open** routes to the Today tab (yesterday's board when the notification's day is yesterday and still open).
- Wire it in `AppEnvironment`. A fake client is used under `-uiTestNotifications fake` (grants) and `-uiTestNotifications fakeDeny` (denies). M3.14 relies on both.
