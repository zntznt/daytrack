## Goal
- `Shortfall(count, threshold, remaining)`.
- The report floor: 14 set values per tracker, in the latest era inside the range.
- The pair floor constant: 12 days on which both trackers are set.
- `builderCanGenerate`: true when at least one selected tracker passes.
- `recordStart`: the first day with anything written.
- `HistoryRanges`: "so far" while the record is under 30 days old, then 30, 90 and "12 months" as each becomes real. 12 months is rolling, not the calendar year. History ranges end today and report ranges end at `closedThrough` **[O9]**.
- The historyEmpty thresholds: 1, 2 and 12 days.

## Why it matters
Every "not enough" message must state a count, a reason and what remains (HANDOFF §5 rule 3). They all need one source.
