## Goal
- **Tracker sheets:** one chart per tracker, drawn with Swift Charts. Gaps stay gaps, the revision boundary is marked with its date in the caption, and the reduced n is shown. A tracker below the floor prints its shortfall instead.
- **Pair sheets:** print the lag, "split of the mean, no coefficient, no test" and "chosen after seeing the data".
