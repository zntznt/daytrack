## Goal
- `WeekGrid`: 7 cells per tracker, each `.set`, `.skip`, `.blank` or `.inactive`.
  - The per-tracker delta compares the mean of the days present with the previous week's.
  - A partial week reports "{k} of 7 days so far". The Copy string is added here.
- `WeekdayPanel`: n and mean per weekday, era-windowed. It **never names a winner** **[O4]**.

## Why it matters
These are the Week and Tracker detail screens. They must not repeat design-review Medium 6, Low 3 or Low 4.
