## Goal
Show one past day, read-only:
- every tracker's value, using **that day's** wording: a word or number, "skipped" or "not written yet";
- the note.

Navigation uses one `NavigationStack` per tab, so Back returns to whichever screen opened the day.

## Why it matters
History, Week and Month all lead here. design-review Medium 9 found broken back paths.
