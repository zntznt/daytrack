## Goal
- Every draft change goes through `ReportDrafts` (M6.01). No container writes DeviceState directly.
- Add the **Report** tab. The final tab order is Today, History, Report, Settings (Q7a).
- The builder holds the range, the trackers (each with its era count and a revision marker), the sections and the pairs.
- The thin state states the count, the reason and what remains, and offers a wider range. It never shows a ring or a progress bar.
- Generate is enabled according to `builderCanGenerate`.
- History gets a "Report this range" header action, which carries the range into the draft.
- Compare gets "Add to report · computed over the report's range ({range}), not this one". Once added, it reads "In the report".
