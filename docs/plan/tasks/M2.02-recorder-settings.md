## Goal
- `Recorder`, the board's single entry point. It offers `write`, `skip`, `clear`, `setNote`, `skipRest(day:)` and `refresh()`. Every call:
  1. advances `closedThrough` from the Clock;
  2. applies the pure write;
  3. saves;
  4. calls `WidgetPublishing.publish` and `ReminderScheduling.replan`.

  A write to a closed day throws, and the store stays untouched.
- `SettingsService`:
  - `setLockHour(_:)` returns `.needsConfirm` when lowering the lock hour would close yesterday, and `confirmLockHour(_:)` applies it;
  - `setReminderAt(_:)`;
  - `recordFullCopySaved(at:)`, which writes DeviceState.

## Why it matters
No view touches storage or rules. Every side effect happens in one place, in one order.
