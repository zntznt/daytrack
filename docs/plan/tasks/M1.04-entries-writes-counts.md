## Goal
- `DayValue`: `.set(Double)` or `.skip`. A missing key means blank.
- `DayEntry` with fields `date`, `values`, `note?`, `firstWrittenAt`, `lastWrittenAt`, `tzOffsetAtWrite`, `writtenNextDay`, `origin` (`.device` or `.import`, matching decisions Q3's `'device' | 'import'`) and `importedAt?`.
- Pure write operations: `set`, `skip`, `clear`, `setNote` and `skipRest(active:)`. Each is guarded by `DayStatus` and each throws `.dayClosed`.
- `DayCounts`: written, skipped-only and blank counts for a selected tracker set.

## Why it matters
Keeping blank and skipped apart, and recording where each write came from, is what makes the clinician report honest (rules 3 and 4, and the provenance rule in Q3).
