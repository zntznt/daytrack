## Goal
- `ReportDraft` holds a range, trackers, sections and pairs `[{a, b, lag}]`.
  - Pairs are deduplicated on (a, b, lag).
  - The default range ends at `closedThrough` **[O9]**.
  - The draft is stored in DeviceState, never in the record. This replaces the `Data?` placeholder from M2.01.
- `Provenance` reports two counts over the range:
  - x of n days that were written or changed on the following day;
  - y days that came in by import.

  It also builds the Q3 sentence, verbatim, through `Copy`, and keeps the other §7 sentences: scales are subjective and self-defined, **not validated instruments**, with no clinical units and no equal-interval guarantee; missing days are never imputed; and the revision sentence when the range crosses a boundary. It never prints the lock hour.
- `ReportDrafts`, a small core use case over `DeviceStateStore`: `load`, `setRange`, `toggleTracker`, `toggleSection`, `addPair`. The Report and Compare containers (M6.03) call it; no container writes DeviceState directly.
- `ReportFooter` gives the export date and "self-reported, not a clinical instrument".
- A tracker below the floor gets its `Shortfall` and no chart.

## Why it matters
This keeps the clinician-facing output honest and the dates correct (design-review High 4, Medium 1).
