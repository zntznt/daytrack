## Goal
Settings › Data › "Storage" states:
- what is stored: the days, the notes, and the file size;
- that the store is excluded from iCloud and device backup;
- that updates come from the App Store;
- that nothing in the app uses the network;
- the cost of the exclusion, stated plainly (HANDOFF §8 "Arrives"): a restored or new phone arrives with no days, a saved full copy is the only way across, and deleting the app takes the record with it.

## Why it matters
It is the native replacement for the PWA Offline screen, and it keeps rule 1 visible to the user.
