## Goal
`RestorePlan.make(phone: Record, file: Data, now:)` returns either a refusal or a plan.

**Refusals.** A refusal is `.refused(fileName, reason)`, where reason comes from `CopyFileCodec.decode` or `.csvNotRestorable` **[O2]**.

**The plan.**
- Days missing from the phone come in with `origin = .import` and `importedAt = now`.
- "A day this phone has" means a `DayEntry` exists on the phone. An absent day comes in even when it is at or before `closedThrough`; otherwise a phone move could never bring history across **[O12]**.
- Days present on both but different keep this phone's version, and their dates are listed.
- A day that has an entry on the phone never changes, closed or open.
- An imported day that is still open stays editable.
- `isFresh`: when the phone has nothing to disagree with, no choices are needed.

`apply(plan)` returns the new Record. The file's `closedThrough` and lock hour are ignored, so the phone keeps its own.

## Why it matters
Restore is the only way across phones (Q8). Q3 says nothing changes a closed day, restore included.
