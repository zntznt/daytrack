## Goal
- `Eraser`, a core use case. It clears the record, Settings, `closedThrough` and DeviceState (`lastFullCopyAt`, `reportDraft`, every other field), then calls `ReminderScheduling.removeAll()` and `WidgetPublishing.publish` with the empty record.
- Add `eraseAll()` to the `RecordStore` port. `FileRecordStore.eraseAll()` deletes `record.json`, `device.json` **and every `record-unreadable-*.json`** (an erase that keeps a moved-aside copy would make the Erase copy false). The erase container also deletes `tmp/Exports/`.
- The confirm screen states the real counts: trackers, days and notes. Its copy reads "Daytrack keeps no other copy. Files you saved yourself are not touched; Daytrack cannot see them."
- After erasing, the app shows the Erased screen and then onboarding.

## Why it matters
HANDOFF requires that the record be fully deletable. Q2 amendment 2 corrects the copy. The MVP must offer this on the phone.
