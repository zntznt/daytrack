## Goal
Build only what the owner keeps. **If the owner keeps none, close this task with no PR.**
- **Fonts:** bundle Instrument Sans and IBM Plex Mono (OFL), with their licence files. **The owner supplies the font and licence files** (committed to the branch or placed in the Linux checkout). The agent never downloads them: rule 1 and the no-download rule in AGENT_BRIEF. Use `Font.custom(_:size:relativeTo:)`. Not adding them is the default.
- **Accent picker:** show the measured blue-channel figure for each swatch, with the over-ceiling caution.
- **Night shade:** an overlay, 35% by default.

## Why it matters
HANDOFF §4 specifies these, but the Visual decision may have replaced them. They must not ship by accident.
