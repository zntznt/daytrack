## Goal
Add three types:
- `DayKey`: year, month and day. It is Comparable, Hashable and Codable as the string `"YYYY-MM-DD"`.
- `DateRange`: inclusive start and end DayKeys.
- `LocalCalendar`: maps a Date plus a TimeZone to a DayKey, gives the start of a day, adds days, and gives the weekday.

## Why it matters
Every rule keys on local calendar dates, never on offsets from a Date. HANDOFF §6 says "boundaries are absolute dates". Relative offsets caused design-review Medium 1 and Medium 2.

## Read
