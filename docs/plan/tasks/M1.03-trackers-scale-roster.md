## Goal
Add these types:
- `TrackerType` (numeric, ordinal, binary) and its `stops` (11, 5, 2).
- The canonical 0–10 encoding: `value(forIndex:)` and `index(forValue:)`.
- `Tracker` with fields `id` (UUID), `name`, `symbol` (an SF Symbol name), `type`, `labels`, `anchors?`, `createdAt: DayKey` and `archivedRanges: [{from, to?}]`. `isArchived` is derived from those ranges.
- `ActiveSpan.isActive(tracker, on:)`.
- Default labels.
- `StarterCatalog`: templates, never trackers.
- `Roster.canActivate`: the cap of 8, with the exact refusal copy.

## Why it matters
Every board row, stat and blank count depends on this encoding. Storing the index instead breaks the moment the wording is revised (HANDOFF §2).
