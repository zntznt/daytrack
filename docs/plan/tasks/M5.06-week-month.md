## Goal
- **Week:** 7 columns from `WeekGrid`, with a delta per tracker, "k of 7 days so far" for a partial week, and a legend for skipped and blank.
- **Month:** a calendar heat grid of counts only. It can navigate to any month, including months with nothing written.

Both views lead to Day detail.
