## Goal
- `WidgetFace`, in the core. It builds the entries for now, the next midnight and the next close instant. Each entry carries the day name, the blanks remaining, the dot counts and the lock line from `Copy`. **It never carries a value, a label, a tracker name or a note.** It also provides the accessibility label, for example "three still to write, Mon open until Tue 23:00".
- `SnapshotPublisher: WidgetPublishing`, in the app. It encodes the faces to `widget-snapshot.json` in the App Group container and calls `WidgetCenter.shared.reloadAllTimelines()`. Add the App Group entitlement `group.$(BUNDLE_PREFIX).daytrack` to the app in `project.yml`.

## Why it matters
Privacy: a home screen is read by other people (HANDOFF §3, §8). The widget process never sees the record.
