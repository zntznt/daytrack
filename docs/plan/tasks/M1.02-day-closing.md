## Rules
- Day D closes at the first local instant at or after lockHour:00 on D+1. When a DST gap removes that hour, use the next instant that exists: `Calendar.nextDate(... matchingPolicy: .nextTime)`.
- lockHour 0 closes D at its own midnight, so there is no grace day.
- `closedThrough` never moves backward. Raising the lock hour or travelling west never reopens a day.
- A day after `closedThrough` is open only if its close instant is still in the future.
