## Goal
- Settings › Data › "Other formats" offers a CSV export through `.fileExporter`, labelled as lossy. The DEBUG `-uiTestExportTo` bypass applies here too.
- Settings › Data › "Move to a new phone" opens a three-step explainer: save a full copy, move the file, then restore on the new phone. It says that the old phone keeps its days and that days written later on the old phone are not carried over. It links to Save a full copy.

## Why it matters
Q2 (CSV is documented as lossy) and Q8. Q8 replaces QR pairing.
