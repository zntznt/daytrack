## Goal
Today gets one strip slot, driven by `BoardStrip`. It is a plain glass strip, never an alert, and nothing is displaced from the amend slot.
- An **Amend** action appears only when the strip is amendable.
- A **"Why it was held"** action explains Focus and Scheduled Summary, then Back returns to Today.
- The lapse strip states the gap stays blank ("There is no backlog"), and offers "Just do today" and "Track fewer things" (the Settings › Trackers route from M3.15), plus Amend while yesterday is open (design-review Medium 14).
- The amend chip stays visible while any strip shows (design-review Medium 10).
