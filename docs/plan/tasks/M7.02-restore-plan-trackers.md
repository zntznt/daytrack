## Goal
Extend `RestorePlan` with tracker conflicts:
- Trackers match by id.
- A stop-count mismatch, or a wording conflict on the same id, brings the file's tracker in as a **separate archived tracker**, with its days attached.
- Revisions from an older file merge in as absolute boundaries. No value is converted.
- If the merge would leave more than 8 trackers active, the extras come in archived, and the plan exposes `needsActiveChoice`. `apply(choices:)` accepts at most 8 active trackers.

## Why it matters
A silent re-score on import is "the single worst bug" (HANDOFF §8).
