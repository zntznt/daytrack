## Goal
- A Settings reminder row with a toggle and a time. The default is 21:30.
- Notification permission is requested **only** after an explicit tap on the toggle, or on "Remind me at 21:30" on the day-one-written screen. It is asked once.
- When permission is denied, Settings shows "Notifications are off for Daytrack in system settings", and the app never asks again.
- The day-one-written screen appears **once**, never mid-drag: when the first day's board has no blanks left, or on the first launch after a day with anything written, whichever comes first **[O11]**. A DeviceState flag `dayOneScreenShown` records it, so Erase resets it. It shows the "Remind me at 21:30" button and a "No reminder" link. It makes no "asks again" promise.
- Settings states "scheduled for {HH:MM}; delivery can still move it" and the 14-day limit, from `Copy`. When today has no blanks it also reads "Nothing arrives tonight" (HANDOFF §9).
- The reminder screen headline is "Daily reminder" (decisions Q1 amendment 2) and its eyebrow is "AFTER DAY ONE" (Q10).

## Why it matters
Q10: permission is never asked at launch. This completes the MVP habit loop.
