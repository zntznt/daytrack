## Goal
Render the summary and provenance sheet models as SwiftUI views at 612×792 pt.
- Ink on `#fbfaf7`, with amber as a spot accent only. **No glass on paper.**
- The summary header reads "days with anything written" for the selected trackers.
- The provenance sheet prints the Q3 sentence with x and y.
- Every sheet has the footer.

## Why it matters
These are the clinician-facing caveats in §7.
