## Goal
Each day has one note. It is reachable from Today and from Amend, and it saves automatically with no save button (debounced, plus a save on disappear). It stays editable until the day closes. The footer names the same close time as the chip, from `Copy`.

## Why it matters
Notes are part of the daily ritual, the copy file and the report. design-review Low 2 found that the note footer and the chip disagreed.
