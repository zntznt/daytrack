## Goal
When the user has crossed a time zone while a day is still open, Today explains that the day got longer or shorter, and that it keeps its date and closes at the lock hour on the clock in their hand. Nothing is re-dated.

- `TimeZoneNote.make(record:now:tz:)` in `Board/` returns `.none` or `.crossed(day, hoursDelta)`. It fires when today's or open yesterday's `DayEntry.tzOffsetAtWrite` differs from the current offset. No entry means nothing to explain. No new stored state.
- It uses the board strip slot from M8.02 and has the **lowest** precedence: missed and lapse win **[O13]**.
- Copy, in `Copy/StripCopy.swift` **[O13]**, for example: "You changed time zone. {Day} is {n} hours shorter on this clock. It keeps its date and closes at {weekday} {HH}:00 here."

## Why it matters
HANDOFF §9 "Time zone crossing" requires the explanation, and no other task covers it. The rule itself (closing on the local clock, never re-dating) is already tested in M1.02.
