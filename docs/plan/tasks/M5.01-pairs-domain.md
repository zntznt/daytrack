## Goal
`PairResult.make(a, b, lag: 0|1, range, record)` works over days on which both trackers are set. Each tracker is windowed to its own latest era inside the range. It returns one of:
- `.split`: B's mean and n on days where A is above A's mean, and B's mean and n on the other days. "Above" means strictly greater **[O3]**.
- `.shortfall(Shortfall)`: fewer than 12 days.
- `.noSplit`: every A value falls on one side of its mean.

## Why it matters
This is the logic behind the Compare screen and the pair sheet. It must not claim more than the data shows (§6).
