## Goal
`TrackerManager` offers these operations:
- `createFromCatalog` and `createCustom`. Both enforce the cap of 8 and set `createdAt` to today.
- `startBoard(picked:)` for onboarding. It creates only the picked starters.
- `archive` and `bringBack`. Both maintain the archive ranges, and `bringBack` is refused at 8.
- `reorder`, `rename` and `setSymbol`.
- `editWording`, through `WordingEditRule`.
- `deletePreview`, which returns the real count of days that hold a value, and `delete`.

## Why it matters
Onboarding, Settings › Trackers and revisions all go through it. It is the only writer of tracker state.
