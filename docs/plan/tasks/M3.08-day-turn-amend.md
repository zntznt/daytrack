## Goal
- `DayTicker` schedules one task at `DayClosing.nextBoundary`. At midnight it relabels the board; at the lock hour it closes yesterday. Both events are deferred while a finger is down and fire on release. The ticker also refreshes on foreground.
- The amend chip reads "until {weekday} {HH}:00", from `Copy`. It sits in a slot nothing displaces, and it is hidden when lockHour is 0 or yesterday is closed.
- The chip opens yesterday's board: the same rows, with yesterday's wording and "Skip the rest of yesterday".
- Yesterday is read-only after it closes.

## Why it matters
Rule 2's grace day, and design-review High 1, High 2 and Medium 10. A finger on the slider at 23:59:59 must not lose its value.
