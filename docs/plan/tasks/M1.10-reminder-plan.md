## Goal
`ReminderPlan.make(reminderAt:now:record:tz:horizonDays: 14)` returns a list of `(day: DayKey, fire: DateComponents)`. Each item is one non-repeating, floating-time fire: `timeZone` is nil, and the components are year, month, day, hour and minute.

Today is left out when the board has no blanks or when the reminder time has already passed. The plan is empty when `reminderAt` is nil or when no tracker is active. Missed days are never re-fired.

Also add these Copy strings: "scheduled for {HH:MM}; delivery can still move it", and the 14-day limit sentence.

## Why it matters
It keeps the scheduling rules testable without UserNotifications. The adapter in M3.13 becomes a thin loop.
