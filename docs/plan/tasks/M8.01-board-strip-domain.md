## Goal
`BoardStrip.make(record:device:now:)` returns `.none`, `.missed(amendable: Bool)` or `.lapse(amendable: Bool)` **[O1]**:
- **missed:** `remindersScheduledThrough` covers yesterday, a reminder was planned for yesterday, and yesterday is wholly blank. It is amendable while yesterday is open. At lockHour 0 it is never amendable, and the copy says yesterday is closed.
- **lapse:** the last day with anything written is 3 or more days before today. The gap stays blank.
- When both apply, missed wins. There is at most one strip.

Add the Copy strings: "may have been held", and "Why it was held".

## Why it matters
design-review Medium 14 found that the strip trigger and precedence were undefined.
