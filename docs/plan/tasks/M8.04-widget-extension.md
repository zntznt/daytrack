## Goal
- Add the `DaytrackWidget` WidgetKit extension to `project.yml`. It is embedded in the app, shares the App Group, and links `DaytrackCore` for `WidgetFace` decoding only.
- One systemSmall widget. Its TimelineProvider reads only `widget-snapshot.json` and draws the face: day name, blanks remaining, dots and lock line, with `.accessibilityLabel` from the snapshot.
- When no snapshot exists, it shows a neutral "Open Daytrack" state.

## Why it matters
The widget is the native home-screen surface.

Placing a widget on the simulator home screen cannot be scripted reliably, so there is no screenshot gate. The owner checks the widget on the phone after the next `scripts/mac device`.
