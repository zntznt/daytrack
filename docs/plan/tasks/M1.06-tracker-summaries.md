## Goal
`TrackerSummary(tracker, range, record)` (Record from M1.05) is the one era-windowing helper. It reports:
- n: the set values in the latest era inside the range;
- skips, and blanks clipped to the active span and the era window;
- mean, high and low;
- for binary trackers, k of n; for ordinal trackers, the distribution and the median index with n;
- `crossesBoundary`, `windowedRange` and the reduced n.

## Why it matters
Every number on screen and on paper comes from here. design-review High 9 found two different averages on one screen. One helper removes that class of bug.
