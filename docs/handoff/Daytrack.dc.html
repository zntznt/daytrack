Daytrack.

Eight things, one screen, ten seconds. Drag anywhere on a track to set today, or tap the value to mark the day skipped on purpose. At the lock hour the day is written; you get one more day to amend it, then it is read-only for good. Untouched sliders stay honestly blank.

Assumptions
Surfaces and the three default accents are near blue-free (blue channel 1–11% of full; Settings offers three warmer-to-cooler alternatives that exceed it and say so), peak luminance capped, brightest panel is amber parchment rather than white. Text keeps a little blue, around half, because legibility wins over dogma. Night shade multiplies amber over everything for a deeper cut. Symbols are Font Awesome Free solid. Frame 1 is live: drag it and every other frame follows.

DAYTRACK · {{ repSpan }}
how to read this
PROVENANCE
Patient-entered, once daily, on the day itself, not reconstructed at the visit. Entries lock at {{ repLockLabel }} and cannot be edited after. Scales are subjective and self-defined: these are not validated instruments and carry no clinical units; numeric anchors are the patient's own, ordinal spacing is not equal-interval. Missing days are never imputed; n counts values only and +n skip counts deliberate skips, which assert nothing happened rather than nothing written, and enter no mean. {{ repRevLine }}
Numeric
0 – 10, set by hand in one pass. The anchors are the patient's own and are not calibrated against anything.
Ordinal
Absent · Present · Mild · Moderate · Severe, or Yes / No. Spacing between words is not equal-interval; means over them are indicative only.
Missing data
Never imputed, carried forward or estimated. A gap in a line is a day with nothing written.
WHAT THIS RECORD CANNOT TELL YOU
Cause
No figure here is adjusted or tested. Pairs are a split of the mean, chosen after seeing the data.
Anything unrecorded
Medication, travel, illness and season are not in this record unless the patient made a slider for them.
Comparison to others
A 6 here has no meaning outside this record. Only its own movement over time is meaningful.
